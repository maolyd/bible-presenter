This Crown Copyright has no effect outside of the UK, where this work is firmly in the Public Domain.

### Louis Segond 1910 (LSG) — `fra-lsg1910.beblia.xml`
* Text: *La Sainte Bible*, traduction de Louis Segond, version revue 1910.
* Status: **Public domain**.
